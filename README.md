# One Line of Earned Pride

Entry for the make.fun competition
`u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557` — a program that writes one line
of text to `/output/entry.txt`, a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone
in a scene.

Bounty page: https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557

## The text

> He spent three years rebuilding the boat his father sank. When it finally
> floated, he didn't cheer. He just sat on the dock, watching it hold water.

## Why this text

The judge's brief asks for pride that is earned inside the line, by effort
or difficulty the reader can feel — never asserted, congratulated or
explained. This one does that with a single concrete scene: three years of
rebuilding, a boat that once sank, and a man who does not cheer. The pride
arrives in the last words — he just sits and watches it hold water — with no
setup the reader has to assemble, and no stock trophy, podium or graduation
in sight.
